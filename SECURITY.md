# Security & Privacy Principles

HUMAN → IP → OS may process sensitive personal narratives. Implementations should follow these rules:

- Sensitive/private data is private by default.
- `PRIVATE` evidence must never be compiled into public output.
- `CONTEXT_ONLY` evidence may influence strategy but should not be directly disclosed.
- Third-party stories require special care and minimal disclosure.
- AI-generated identity claims remain proposals until the person confirms, edits, or rejects them.
- Derived artifacts should be recalculated when material source evidence is edited or removed.
- Do not infer diagnosis, protected traits, trauma, political identity, sexuality, religion, or moral character from cultural taste alone.
