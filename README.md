# HUMAN → IP → OS

**Know yourself. Express yourself. Build yourself.**

HUMAN → IP → OS is an evidence-based methodology and AI Skill system for understanding a real person before designing a public identity, then turning confirmed identity into a durable personal brand and operating system.

> A person is larger than their brand.

## Skills

### V1.1 — Human Discovery Method

**Know yourself.**

A deep human-discovery Skill that explores six Human DNA dimensions: **Experience, Capability, Curiosity, Value, Taste, and Desire**. It uses progressive interviewing, evidence discipline, Taste & Cultural DNA, privacy boundaries, and human confirmation to understand the person before any brand positioning begins.

Path: [`skills/v1.1-human-discovery`](skills/v1.1-human-discovery)

### V2.1 — Human & IP Diagnostic Engine

**Express yourself.**

An evidence-based personal-IP diagnostic Skill that separates the whole human from the chosen public self. It maintains Evidence and Taste Ledgers, compares multiple positioning hypotheses, exposes proof gaps, protects private/context-only information, requires human confirmation, and compiles validated identity into IP DNA, Brand Genome, and `[NAME]OS`.

Path: [`skills/v2.1-human-ip-diagnostic`](skills/v2.1-human-ip-diagnostic)

## Product architecture

`Human Genome → Human DNA → Public Self → IP DNA → Brand Genome → [NAME]OS`

The broader product direction continues into V3.0 — **Living Human Brand OS**, where positioning becomes action, outcomes become proof, and `[NAME]OS` evolves over time.

## Core principles

- **AI proposes. Evidence explains. Human decides.**
- Human DNA is not the same as IP DNA.
- Taste is explored through meaning, not stereotype.
- Aspiration is not proof.
- Private understanding is not automatic permission to publish.
- Contradictions are retained instead of erased for a cleaner story.
- Strategic claims should remain traceable to source evidence.
