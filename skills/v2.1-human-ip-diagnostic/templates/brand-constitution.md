### Opportunity Filter
Before accepting a topic/project/sponsor:
1. Is it ours to do?
2. Who gets real value?
3. What evidence supports our authority?
4. Does it strengthen the long-term territory?
5. Is there an ethical/privacy cost?
6. Does it create a durable asset?
