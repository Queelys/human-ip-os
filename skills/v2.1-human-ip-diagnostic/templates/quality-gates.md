# V2 Quality Gates

## Gate 0 — Intake Completeness
Enough context to avoid generic diagnosis?

## Gate 1 — Evidence
Are major claims traceable to LIVED / PROOF / REPEATED / ASPIRATIONAL evidence?

## Gate 2 — Specificity
Name Removal Test: still generic?
Competitor Swap Test: could a competitor say exactly this?

## Gate 3 — Positioning
Why this person?
Why this territory?
Why these people?
Why now?

## Gate 4 — Credibility
Is public authority supported by proof rather than aspiration?

## Gate 5 — Narrative Integrity
No invented causality, drama, trauma, credentials or inevitability?

## Gate 6 — Content Durability
Can core themes generate 20+ meaningful non-repetitive works?

## Gate 7 — Action Substance
Would signature action matter without content coverage?

## Gate 8 — Commercial Alignment
Does monetization preserve trust and mission?

## Gate 9 — AI Accountability
Every AI role has input/output/reviewer/failure boundary?

## Gate 10 — Website Comprehension
30 seconds:
Who?
What matters?
What is made/done?
Why continue?
What next?

## Gate 11 — Launch Feasibility
Can the 90-day plan be executed without destroying the person's life?

## Blocking Conditions
Authenticity ≤2
Credibility ≤2
Human usefulness ≤2
unresolved ethics/privacy issue
aspiration presented as proof
