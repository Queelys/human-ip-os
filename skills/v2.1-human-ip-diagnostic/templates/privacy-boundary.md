# Privacy / Public Self Boundary

For each strategic signal:

- PRIVATE
- CONTEXT_ONLY
- PUBLIC_CANDIDATE
- PUBLIC_CONFIRMED

## Public Self Review
- What do I want the world to know?
- What may shape strategy but should stay private?
- Which stories involve other people's privacy?
- Which claims am I comfortable being held accountable for publicly?
- Which future aspirations should remain framed as experiments rather than authority?
