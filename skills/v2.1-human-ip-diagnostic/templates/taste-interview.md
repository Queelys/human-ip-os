# Taste & Culture Interview

Use progressive prompt cards, not a long form.

Possible cards:
- 3 memorable films
- childhood animation/anime
- 5 books to keep
- music you return to
- admired person you do not want to imitate
- character that stays with you
- city/space that feels like you
- brand whose world you love
- 5 “I don't know why, I just love it” things

For each:
1. What exactly attracts you?
2. Which detail remains?
3. What does it mean to you?
4. Would you still like it without that quality?
5. Where else does this attraction appear?

End with reflection, not diagnosis.
