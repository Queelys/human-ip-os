# Client Session Protocol

## Session 1 — Discovery
Goal: evidence, not positioning.
Output: source map + missing evidence.

## Session 2 — Diagnosis
Goal: patterns, tensions, diagnostic profile.
Output: 2–4 hypotheses.

## Session 3 — Strategic Choice
Goal: discuss trade-offs and choose/merge direction.
Output: selected IP DNA.

## Session 4 — Constitution & Narrative
Goal: codify identity and boundaries.

## Session 5 — Content + Action
Goal: content universe and signature project.

## Session 6 — Business + OS
Goal: value flywheel and Human × AI system.

## Session 7 — Public Interface
Goal: website/public presence.

## Session 8 — 90-Day Launch
Goal: turn strategy into execution.

Sessions may be combined for simple cases.
Never force the client through ceremony when evidence is already sufficient.
