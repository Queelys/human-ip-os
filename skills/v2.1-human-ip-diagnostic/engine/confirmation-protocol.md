# Human Confirmation Protocol

## Applies to
- Motif
- Tension
- PositioningHypothesis
- public identity claims
- important Brand Genome fields

## States
AI_PROPOSED | USER_CONFIRMED | USER_EDITED | USER_REJECTED

## Interaction
AI should say:
“I notice a possible pattern across [sources]: __. This is only a hypothesis. Does it feel true?”

User can:
CONFIRM | EDIT | REJECT | NOT SURE

## Rule
AI_PROPOSED objects may support exploration.
They must not silently become confirmed public identity.
