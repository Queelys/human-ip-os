# Evidence Ledger

| Evidence ID | Raw Evidence | Class | Domain | Strength 1–5 | Recency | Public? | Strategic Implication | Uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|---|---|

Classes:
- LIVED
- PROOF
- REPEATED
- ASPIRATIONAL

## Rules
- One evidence item may support multiple hypotheses.
- Aspirations can define direction but not current authority.
- Mark contradictions; do not delete them.
- Sensitive/private evidence may inform strategy without appearing in public copy.
