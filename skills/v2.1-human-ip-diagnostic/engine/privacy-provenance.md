# Privacy & Provenance Engine

## Visibility
PRIVATE | CONTEXT_ONLY | PUBLIC_CANDIDATE | PUBLIC_CONFIRMED

## Provenance
Every strategic object should retain `source_ids[]`.

## Public compiler rules
- PRIVATE: prohibited from direct public output.
- CONTEXT_ONLY: may influence strategy but must not be disclosed directly.
- PUBLIC_CANDIDATE: requires review.
- PUBLIC_CONFIRMED: eligible for public Brand Genome.

## Sensitive third-party stories
Minimize detail and require explicit permission before public use.

## Deletion/editing
Derived objects should be recalculated when material source evidence is edited, rejected or removed.
