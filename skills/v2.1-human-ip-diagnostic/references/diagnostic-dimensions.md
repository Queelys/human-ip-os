# Diagnostic Dimensions

Score 1–5. Always cite evidence IDs.

1. Identity Clarity
2. Expertise Proof
3. Lived Distinctiveness
4. Curiosity Depth
5. Audience Proximity
6. Problem Specificity
7. Point-of-View Strength
8. Story Energy
9. Creation Capacity
10. Action Capacity
11. Network / Resource Leverage
12. Commercial Readiness
13. Public Expression Readiness
14. AI Leverage Potential

## Interpretation
1 = insufficient evidence / weak
2 = emerging
3 = usable
4 = strong
5 = unusually strong

A low score is not a defect in the person.
It tells the strategist what must be learned, built or proven.
