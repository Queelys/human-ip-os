# Functional Archetype System

Archetypes describe audience relationship and behavior.

## Explorer
Finds new territory; risk: endless exploration.

## Teacher
Makes knowledge usable; risk: over-explaining / generic education.

## Builder
Turns ideas into systems/products; risk: output without meaning.

## Guide
Helps people navigate change; risk: authority without proof.

## Connector
Links people/domains/resources; risk: weak owned point of view.

## Creator
Transforms ideas into works; risk: aesthetics without utility.

## Researcher
Builds evidence and understanding; risk: inaccessible complexity.

## Challenger
Questions defaults; risk: contrarianism for attention.

## Caregiver
Centers human wellbeing; risk: over-responsibility / boundary issues.

## Curator
Selects and contextualizes; risk: derivative identity.

## Founder
Mobilizes resources around a mission; risk: brand becomes company PR.

## Craftsperson
Embodies mastery and process; risk: narrow discoverability.

## Documentarian
Makes reality visible through stories; risk: subject exploitation.

## Host / Convener
Creates spaces and relationships; risk: identity depends on guests.

Use 1 primary + up to 2 supporting when useful.
