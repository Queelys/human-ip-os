## V2.1 Upgrade — Human & IP Diagnostic Engine

V2.1 keeps the complete V2.0 client-operable system and adds a formal understanding layer before public positioning.

New architecture:

`Light Intake → Adaptive Interview → Taste & Culture Interview → Evidence Ledger + Taste Ledger → Dynamic Human DNA → Tension Finder → Positioning Lab → Human Confirmation → Public Self → IP DNA → Brand Genome → [NAME]OS`

### Human DNA Dimensions

- `EXPERIENCE`
- `CAPABILITY`
- `CURIOSITY`
- `VALUE`
- `TASTE`
- `DESIRE`

These dimensions describe strategic understanding, not human worth.

### Evidence Classes

Keep the V2 evidence classes:
- `LIVED`
- `PROOF`
- `REPEATED`
- `ASPIRATIONAL`

Rule:
`ASPIRATIONAL` may express direction, but may not be presented as present authority.

### Taste Ledger

Taste evidence must retain:
- source object
- category
- attraction
- user meaning
- proposed motif
- recurrence
- source IDs
- counter-evidence
- confidence
- confirmation state
- visibility

Never infer psychology directly from a title or category.

Use:
`Object → Attraction → User Meaning → Repeated Motif → Possible Signal → Human Confirmation`

### Confirmation States

Derived strategic objects must use:
- `AI_PROPOSED`
- `USER_CONFIRMED`
- `USER_EDITED`
- `USER_REJECTED`

AI-generated motifs, tensions and positioning claims are proposals until confirmed.

### Visibility States

Use:
- `PRIVATE`
- `CONTEXT_ONLY`
- `PUBLIC_CANDIDATE`
- `PUBLIC_CONFIRMED`

`PRIVATE` and `CONTEXT_ONLY` evidence must not appear directly in public Brand Genome fields.

### Positioning Lab

Generate 2–4 genuinely different paths when ambiguity is meaningful.

For each path include:
- working title
- one-line position
- core question
- audience
- problem / tension
- unique combination
- proof base
- proof gaps
- functional archetype
- content potential
- action potential
- business potential
- risks
- 5-year durability
- source IDs

Do not silently select a winner.
The person may select, edit, merge or reject.

### Future Simulation

For each viable positioning path, simulate coherent implications:
- homepage hero
- 3 content pillars
- signature action
- first offer
- first 90-day proof mission
- what the path intentionally excludes

This is strategic scenario exploration, not a prediction of success.

### Public Self Gate

Before compiling IP DNA:
- key motifs/tensions have been reviewed;
- important public fields are `PUBLIC_CONFIRMED`;
- authority claims have adequate `PROOF`;
- proof gaps are visible;
- private/context-only sources are not directly disclosed;
- the person understands trade-offs and confirms the selected direction.

### Action & Proof Bridge

When a desired positioning lacks proof, do not compensate with louder content.

Use:
`ASPIRATIONAL → Proof Gap → Mission → Action → Outcome → PROOF`

New proof should be returned to the Evidence Ledger for future re-diagnosis.

# Operating Rule

Never jump from questionnaire answers directly to a slogan.

Every strategic claim must be supported by evidence.

Use four evidence classes:

- `LIVED` — lived experience
- `PROOF` — demonstrated outcome / credential / artifact
- `REPEATED` — repeated interest, behavior or demand
- `ASPIRATIONAL` — desired future, not yet proven

Do not present ASPIRATIONAL evidence as current authority.

# Phase 0 — Client Intake

Use `templates/client-intake.md`.

Collect enough context to decide what not to ask again.

Do not treat intake as truth without interpretation.
Tag every meaningful statement in the Evidence Ledger.

# Phase 1 — Evidence Ledger

Use `engine/evidence-ledger.md`.

For each evidence item record:
- ID
- raw evidence
- class: LIVED / PROOF / REPEATED / ASPIRATIONAL
- domain
- recency
- strength 1–5
- public/private
- strategic implication
- uncertainty

Never expose private evidence in public deliverables without explicit permission.

# Phase 2 — Diagnostic Profile

Score dimensions 1–5 using evidence, not vibes:

1. Identity Clarity
2. Expertise Proof
3. Lived Distinctiveness
4. Curiosity Depth
5. Audience Proximity
6. Problem Specificity
7. Point-of-View Strength
8. Story Energy
9. Creation Capacity
10. Action Capacity
11. Network / Resource Leverage
12. Commercial Readiness
13. Public Expression Readiness
14. AI Leverage Potential

Scores are diagnostic, not judgments of human worth.

For every score:
- cite evidence IDs;
- explain confidence;
- list missing evidence.

Do not calculate a single “personal brand score.”

# Phase 3 — Tension Finder

Strong brands often emerge from meaningful tensions.

Look for:
- old identity vs new identity
- expertise vs curiosity
- local vs global
- tradition vs future
- technology vs humanity
- career vs life
- insider vs outsider
- commercial success vs public benefit
- analytical vs artistic
- authority vs beginner's mind

Do not invent tension.

Output:
- observed tension
- evidence
- why it matters
- possible narrative/brand energy
- risk of overclaiming

# Phase 4 — Positioning Hypotheses

Generate 2–4 hypotheses only when ambiguity is meaningful.

Each hypothesis must include:

- working name
- one-sentence position
- core question
- people served
- problem/tension
- unique combination
- proof base
- missing proof
- archetype
- content potential
- action potential
- business potential
- risks
- 5-year durability

Use `templates/positioning-hypothesis-matrix.md`.

Do not silently choose a winner when evidence is close.
Explain trade-offs and invite human choice.

# Phase 5 — Archetype Derivation

Archetypes are descriptive, not horoscope labels.

Derive from behavior and desired relationship with audience.

Candidate functional archetypes:
- Explorer
- Teacher
- Builder
- Guide
- Connector
- Creator
- Researcher
- Challenger
- Caregiver
- Curator
- Founder
- Craftsperson
- Documentarian
- Host / Convener

Prefer 1 primary + up to 2 supporting archetypes.

For each:
- evidence
- audience relationship
- strengths
- shadow risk

# Phase 7 — Narrative System

Create:
1. 30-second introduction
2. 2-minute origin story
3. long-form narrative spine
4. 3–5 life chapters
5. turning points
6. open question / unfinished future

Do not manufacture trauma or heroic inevitability.

# Phase 8 — Brand Constitution

Use `templates/brand-constitution.md`.

This is the highest-order instruction layer for future humans and AI.

It must be capable of rejecting:
- off-brand sponsorship,
- shallow viral content,
- unsupported authority claims,
- unethical storytelling,
- trend-chasing that dilutes the long-term territory.

# Quality Gates

Use `templates/quality-gates.md`.

Blocking failures:
- Authenticity ≤ 2
- Credibility ≤ 2
- Human Usefulness ≤ 2
- ethical conflict unresolved
- public positioning depends primarily on ASPIRATIONAL evidence
- signature action exploits vulnerable people
- website strategy exists before brand comprehension is stable

# Anti-Generic Engine

Reject or revise language dominated by:
- “赋能”
- “成长”
- “热爱”
- “干货”
- “AI时代”
- “链接美好”
- “做更好的自己”
- “长期主义”

unless concrete evidence and meaning make the phrase specific.

Run:
1. Name Removal Test
2. Competitor Swap Test
3. Evidence Trace Test
4. 5-Year Test
5. Action Test

# Human Agency

The engine proposes hypotheses.
The person chooses identity.

Never tell a client:
“This is who you are.”

Prefer:
“The evidence currently supports this interpretation. Here are the alternatives and trade-offs.”

# Versioning

Personal brands evolve.

Every `[NAME]OS` should have:
- version
- date
- evidence changes
- decisions changed
- unchanged constitution elements
- experiments to run next

Recommend re-diagnosis after:
- major career transition
- major audience change
- new product/business
- 6–12 months of meaningful new proof

# Final Principle

Do not manufacture a persona.

Discover what is meaningfully true.
Make it legible.
Make it useful.
Prove it through action.
Build systems that let it compound.
