# Example: Generic → Specific

## Weak
“AI时代个人成长博主，分享AI工具、效率和生活方式。”

Problems:
- interchangeable
- trend-dependent
- no lived proof
- no specific human problem
- no action

## Better discovery result
“Former operations leader who spent a decade coordinating frontline teams and now helps small service-business owners turn undocumented know-how into simple AI-supported operating systems.”

Why stronger:
- lived context
- specific people
- concrete problem
- clear transformation
- AI is a lever, not identity
- creates products, cases, and works

This example is illustrative only.
