# Quality Gates

## Gate 1 — Human specificity
Can this description fit thousands of unrelated people?
If yes: return to discovery.

## Gate 2 — Positioning
Can we answer:
Why this person?
Why this subject?
Why these people?
Why now?

## Gate 3 — Narrative integrity
Did we invent drama, certainty, credentials, or causality?
If yes: repair.

## Gate 4 — Constitution
Can it reject a tempting but wrong opportunity?

## Gate 5 — Content durability
Can each pillar produce at least 20 non-repetitive meaningful works?

## Gate 6 — Action substance
Would the signature project matter without cameras/content?

## Gate 7 — AI accountability
Does every AI role have:
input / output / reviewer / failure boundary?

## Gate 8 — Website comprehension
Within 30 seconds:
Who?
What matters?
What is made/done?
Why continue?
What next?

## Gate 9 — Build readiness
Can a coding agent start without inventing product strategy?

# Blocking Quality Scores
Score 1–5:
- Authenticity
- Distinctiveness
- Credibility
- Human usefulness
- Narrative energy
- Creative potential
- Actionability
- Durability
- Business sustainability
- System coherence

Authenticity ≤2: BLOCK
Credibility ≤2: BLOCK
Human usefulness ≤2: BLOCK
