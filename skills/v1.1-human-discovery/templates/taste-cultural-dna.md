# Taste & Cultural DNA Interview

Use as a conversation, not a checklist.

## Prompt cards
Choose only a few at a time:
- 3 films you still remember
- animation/anime from childhood
- 5 books you would keep
- music you return to
- a game/world you became absorbed in
- a person you admire but do not want to imitate
- a fictional or historical character that stays with you
- a city or space that feels strangely like you
- a brand whose world you love
- an object you keep for reasons beyond utility
- 5 things you “don't know why, but just love”
- a recent fascination
- a childhood obsession

## For each object
Ask:
1. What exactly attracts you?
2. Which detail stays with you?
3. What does it mean to you?
4. Would you still love it without that quality?
5. Where else does this attraction appear?

## Extraction
Record:
- object
- category
- attraction
- user meaning
- source quote / paraphrase
- possible motif
- recurrence
- counter-evidence
- confidence
- confirmation state
- visibility

## Safety
Do not infer:
- diagnosis
- personality type
- political identity
- trauma
- sexuality
- religion
- moral character
from cultural preference alone.

A motif becomes strategically meaningful only when repeated, supported and user-confirmed.
