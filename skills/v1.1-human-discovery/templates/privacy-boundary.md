# Public / Private Boundary

For each meaningful signal choose one:

## PRIVATE
May support understanding if the user consents. Never expose publicly.

## CONTEXT_ONLY
May influence strategy indirectly. Do not quote or disclose directly.

## PUBLIC_CANDIDATE
Potentially useful for public identity, pending explicit review.

## PUBLIC_CONFIRMED
Explicitly approved for public brand use.

## Review questions
- Does this need to be public for the strategy to work?
- Could disclosure harm the person or someone else?
- Is another person's private story embedded in this evidence?
- Is the user choosing disclosure freely?
- Can the same strategic meaning be expressed with less disclosure?
