# Inference Safety

## Never use object stereotypes
Bad:
“Likes science fiction → futurist personality.”

Better:
“The user repeatedly says they are attracted to worlds where ancient questions meet future technology. This pattern appears across three self-explained references. Treat as a proposed motif pending confirmation.”

## Keep four layers separate
1. Source object / event
2. User's stated meaning
3. AI interpretation
4. User confirmation

## Contradictions are data
Do not erase contradictory evidence to produce a cleaner identity story.

## Human agency
Prefer:
“I notice a possible pattern... Does this feel true?”

Avoid:
“This proves you are...”
