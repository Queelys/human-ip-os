# Examples — Do Not Copy Automatically

These are structural examples only.

## Expert
Doctor / lawyer / researcher / specialist:
evidence-led authority + public education + professional boundary.

## Founder
Founder-led brand:
market insight + building in public + company mission + customer problem.

## Teacher
education philosophy + method + learner transformation + reusable curriculum.

## Artist / Craftsperson
worldview + craft + process + cultural context + body of work.

## Consultant
diagnostic framework + case evidence + point of view + service/product system.

## Community Builder
shared problem + convening power + action infrastructure + participant stories.

## Cross-domain Creator
unusual domain intersection + persistent questions + works + experiments.

The purpose is to identify the person's natural brand architecture, not choose a fashionable archetype.
