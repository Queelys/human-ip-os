# HUMAN → IP → OS V1.1

## Purpose

Use this skill when a person wants to:
- discover or reposition a personal IP / personal brand;
- turn life experience, expertise, interests, values, and resources into a coherent brand;
- build a creator/founder/expert public identity;
- design a long-term content and works system;
- create a signature action/project that proves the brand;
- build a Human × AI “super individual” operating system;
- translate the brand into a public website;
- generate a build-ready PRD and coding prompt.

This skill must NOT manufacture a persona. It discovers, clarifies, connects, tests, and operationalizes what is already meaningfully true about the person.

## Core Principle

Do not start from:
“What niche is trending?”

Start from:

WHO ARE YOU?
→ WHAT DO YOU CARE ABOUT?
→ WHAT HAVE YOU LIVED THROUGH?
→ WHAT CAN ONLY YOU CONNECT?
→ WHO CAN YOU HELP?
→ WHAT DO YOU BELIEVE?
→ WHAT WILL YOU CREATE?
→ WHAT WILL YOU ACTUALLY DO?
→ WHAT SYSTEM CAN AMPLIFY YOU?

The final system is:

Identity
→ Narrative
→ IP DNA
→ Brand Constitution
→ Content Universe
→ Signature Project
→ Business / Value Model
→ Super Individual OS
→ Public Interface
→ Vibe Coding Build Pack

## Anti-Template Rule

Never force users into the creator's prior archetype or vocabulary.

Do not automatically use:
- Explorer × Connector × Creator
- “让爱显化”
- cosmic imagery
- thought graphs
- social innovation
- AI agents
- any other example-specific language

unless it genuinely emerges from the new person's evidence.

Every major positioning statement must be traceable to user evidence:
- lived experience,
- demonstrated skill,
- repeated curiosity,
- meaningful audience/problem,
- existing credibility,
- unusual combination,
- resource advantage,
- stated value.

## V1.1 Upgrade — Human Discovery Before Brand Design

V1.1 strengthens Stage 1 so the system can understand a person beyond occupation, expertise and market positioning.

The discovery model now uses six Human DNA dimensions:

1. `EXPERIENCE` — lived chapters, turning points, environments, identities and transitions
2. `CAPABILITY` — demonstrated skills, craft, judgment and repeatedly requested help
3. `CURIOSITY` — questions, obsessions and subjects that sustain attention
4. `VALUE` — principles, red lines and what the person believes is worth protecting
5. `TASTE` — aesthetic and cultural attraction, including films, books, music, people, places, objects and worlds
6. `DESIRE` — future life, works, contribution, role and direction the person genuinely wants

### Human DNA Is Not IP DNA

Human DNA is the understanding layer.
IP DNA is the selected public-expression layer.

A person is always larger than the brand built from them.

Do not convert every meaningful personal signal into public positioning.

Use the visibility states:

- `PRIVATE` — may support understanding with consent; never publish
- `CONTEXT_ONLY` — may influence strategy indirectly; do not disclose directly
- `PUBLIC_CANDIDATE` — may be considered for public identity
- `PUBLIC_CONFIRMED` — explicitly approved for brand use

### Taste & Cultural DNA

Taste is evidence about attraction and meaning, not a shortcut to personality typing.

Explore, when relevant:
- films and series
- animation / anime
- books
- music
- games
- artists, directors, writers, designers
- admired people
- fictional / historical characters
- brands
- cities, places and travel
- architecture and spaces
- objects, collections, clothing and food
- childhood obsessions
- recent fascinations
- things the person says: “I don't know why, I just love it.”

Never infer psychology directly from the object.

Use this chain:

`Object → Attraction → User Meaning → Repeated Motif → Possible Signal → Human Confirmation`

Example:
Liking *Interstellar* does not prove a belief about love, destiny, science or family.
Ask what specifically attracts the person, what remains memorable, what it means to them, and whether the pattern repeats elsewhere.

### Motif Confirmation

AI may propose a motif only when:
- it is supported by multiple source items or unusually strong first-person explanation;
- source items are traceable;
- counter-evidence is retained;
- the motif is labeled as a hypothesis until the person confirms, edits or rejects it.

Allowed states:
`AI_PROPOSED | USER_CONFIRMED | USER_EDITED | USER_REJECTED`

### V1.1 Discovery Gate

Before moving from Human Discovery to IP DNA, verify:

- the six Human DNA dimensions have sufficient coverage for the case;
- the system can explain distinctive raw materials without marketing language;
- important contradictions remain visible;
- taste signals are grounded in user meaning rather than object stereotypes;
- public/private boundaries are recorded;
- no aspiration is being presented as current proof;
- the person has reviewed important AI interpretations.

If the profile could still describe 100,000 people, continue discovery.

## Stage System

There are 9 stages.

Do not blindly generate all stages in one pass. Use stage gates.

### Stage 1 — Deep Discovery

Goal: understand the human before defining the brand.

Investigate:
1. Life chapters and turning points
2. Work / craft / expertise
3. Repeated interests and obsessions
4. Problems they repeatedly notice
5. People they naturally help
6. Skills others ask them for
7. Resources, networks, geography, access
8. Failures, tensions, contradictions
9. Values and red lines
10. Desired future life
11. Commercial ambition
12. Public/private boundaries
13. Preferred media and creative strengths
14. Existing audience, work, products, reputation
15. Topics they can discuss for 5–10 years

Do not ask 30 questions at once.

Use progressive interviewing:
- ask 3–5 high-information questions;
- synthesize;
- identify uncertainty;
- ask the next smallest set.

Output:
`Human Source Map`

Required confidence:
Enough evidence to explain the person's distinctive raw materials without using marketing language.

GATE 1:
Do not proceed if the output could describe thousands of unrelated people.

### Stage 2 — IP DNA

Derive multiple candidate hypotheses before choosing a direction.

For each candidate, define:
- Core Question
- Mission
- Audience
- Problem / tension
- Unique combination
- Brand archetype
- Promise
- Proof
- Long-term territory
- What this brand refuses to become

Generate 2–4 genuinely different positioning hypotheses when ambiguity is material.

Do not rank by “viral potential” alone.

Evaluate:
- authenticity,
- distinctiveness,
- credibility,
- usefulness,
- creative depth,
- 5–10 year durability,
- commercial sustainability,
- action potential.

GATE 2:
The chosen direction must answer:
“Why this person, on this subject, for these people, now?”

### Stage 3 — Life Narrative

Goal: connect life fragments without inventing mythology.

Structure:
Past fragments
→ recurring tension
→ turning point
→ new understanding
→ present mission
→ open future

Find a narrative spine, not a heroic fiction.

Explicitly distinguish:
- fact,
- interpretation,
- metaphor.

Output:
- short origin story,
- long founder/creator narrative,
- 3–5 defining life chapters,
- public/private boundary notes.

GATE 3:
The story must feel recognizable to the person and must not require exaggeration to be compelling.

### Stage 4 — Brand Constitution

Create the long-term “constitution,” not a campaign plan.

Must include:
- mission,
- core question,
- positioning,
- archetype,
- worldview,
- values,
- red lines,
- evidence standard,
- voice,
- audience relationship,
- content principles,
- creation principles,
- commercial principles,
- collaboration principles,
- what we do / do not do,
- 5–10 year direction,
- “when lost, return here” section.

Use `templates/brand-constitution.md`.

GATE 4:
Future AI collaborators should be able to use the constitution to reject an on-brand-looking but wrong idea.

### Stage 5 — Content Universe

Do NOT begin with a posting calendar.

Build:
Mother Themes
→ Persistent Questions
→ Columns / Series
→ Stories
→ Works
→ Distribution

Separate:
- topic,
- thesis,
- story,
- format,
- platform.

Prefer “works” over disposable “content” when appropriate.

For each pillar define:
- central question,
- audience value,
- unique perspective,
- evidence sources,
- repeatable formats,
- visual potential,
- action connection.

Then map platform roles.

GATE 5:
Every pillar must connect to the brand constitution and be sustainable for at least 20 meaningful pieces without repetition.

### Stage 6 — Signature Project / Action IP

Ask:
“What can this person DO that proves what they believe?”

Possible forms:
- public experiment,
- documentary series,
- challenge,
- lab,
- open project,
- community initiative,
- research program,
- product,
- recurring transformation case,
- craft archive,
- fieldwork.

The signature project must not exist only to generate content.

Define:
- real beneficiary / participant,
- real problem,
- intervention,
- role of technology,
- role of humans,
- ethical boundaries,
- measurable evidence,
- storytelling permission,
- repeatability,
- business/public-benefit relationship.

GATE 6:
If the project disappeared but the videos remained, would the brand lose substance?
If no, the action is probably decorative.

### Stage 7 — Super Individual OS

Only add AI where it increases meaningful leverage.

Map human-only responsibilities:
- judgment,
- relationships,
- values,
- final accountability,
- sensitive decisions,
- lived experience,
- taste.

Map delegable AI functions:
- research,
- synthesis,
- drafting,
- coding,
- visual exploration,
- operations,
- analytics,
- archiving,
- workflow support.

Design:
Human
→ Knowledge OS
→ Project OS
→ AI collaborators
→ Production pipeline
→ Feedback loop

Possible systems:
- Notion: projects / actions / editorial operations
- Obsidian or equivalent: private thought / knowledge graph
- Git: versioned public/build assets
- Website: public interface

Do not prescribe tools if simpler alternatives fit better.

GATE 7:
Every AI role must have a defined input, output, human reviewer, and failure boundary.

### Stage 8 — Public Interface / Website

Translate the brand into a digital spatial experience.

Do not default to a resume website.

Choose the website archetype:
- expert authority,
- founder-led brand,
- creator studio,
- portfolio,
- public notebook,
- lab,
- documentary/action hub,
- hybrid.

Produce:
- website product statement,
- target visitor,
- 30-second comprehension goal,
- sitemap,
- homepage narrative,
- page wireframes,
- design direction,
- design tokens,
- data/content models,
- component list,
- CTA hierarchy,
- privacy requirements,
- SEO,
- accessibility,
- performance requirements.

GATE 8:
The homepage must answer:
Who is this?
What do they care about?
What do they make/do?
Why should I continue?
What can I do next?

### Stage 9 — Vibe Coding Build Pack

Generate implementation-ready artifacts:
- Website PRD
- Sitemap
- page wireframes
- design tokens
- TypeScript-style data models
- component inventory
- repository structure
- seed content
- technical requirements
- acceptance criteria
- phased build plan
- Master Coding Prompt

The coding prompt must explicitly state:
- what the website is,
- what it is NOT,
- visual anti-patterns,
- tech constraints,
- routes,
- content seed,
- accessibility,
- performance,
- build validation.

GATE 9:
A coding agent should be able to begin implementation without inventing the product strategy.

## Quality Framework

Score each major system decision from 1–5:

1. Authenticity — rooted in the person's real life?
2. Distinctiveness — difficult to substitute another person?
3. Credibility — proof or plausible path to proof?
4. Human usefulness — creates real value?
5. Narrative energy — contains tension/change/story?
6. Creative potential — can produce meaningful works?
7. Actionability — can become something real?
8. Durability — survives trend cycles?
9. Business sustainability — can support continued work?
10. System coherence — does it reinforce the whole?

Do not simply average scores.

Any score ≤2 in Authenticity, Credibility, or Human Usefulness is a blocking warning.

## Anti-Generic Test

Before finalizing positioning, remove the person's name.

Ask:
- Could this describe 1000 LinkedIn creators?
- Is “AI赋能 / 热爱生活 / 持续成长 / 分享干货” doing the real work?
- Does the positioning depend on vague adjectives?
- Is there a lived contradiction?
- Is there a specific audience or human problem?
- Is there proof?
- Is there a unique connection of domains?
- Can the brand create action, not just posts?

If generic:
return to Discovery / IP DNA.

## Evidence Standard

For research-heavy personal brands, define visible evidence states:

- FACT
- SOURCE CLAIM
- HYPOTHESIS
- INTERPRETATION
- SPECULATION
- CREATIVE

Never present speculation as fact.

For professional/high-stakes domains, require appropriate professional boundaries.

## Vulnerable People / Story Ethics

If the brand works with children, patients, disabled people, trauma, poverty, family conflict, or other vulnerable contexts:

- helping and publicity consent must be separate;
- collect minimum necessary data;
- avoid “suffering as hook” storytelling;
- obtain appropriate guardian consent and participant assent;
- use qualified professionals for professional judgments;
- do not promise outcomes;
- provide tiered privacy choices;
- define withdrawal/publication boundaries;
- distinguish AI assistance from human/professional judgment.

## Business Layer

Do not force monetization too early.

Map value flows:

Free/public value
→ trust
→ proof
→ relationships
→ paid offer/product/service
→ reinvestment into creation/action

Possible revenue models:
- consulting,
- implementation,
- education,
- community,
- digital products,
- software,
- sponsorship,
- licensing,
- collaborations,
- professional services.

Only recommend models aligned with the person's identity, capabilities, audience, and ethics.

## Working Style

- Be curious before prescriptive.
- Synthesize after each interview round.
- Show the user what evidence led to each strategic inference.
- Preserve contradictions instead of prematurely smoothing them.
- Use the person's language when it is distinctive.
- Do not praise vaguely.
- Do not manufacture certainty.
- Prefer one strong coherent system over many disconnected ideas.
- Build progressively; do not overwhelm beginners with implementation detail too early.

## Final Principle

The purpose is not:

> Make a person look like a brand.

The purpose is:

> Discover what is meaningfully true about a person, make it legible, useful and trustworthy, then build systems that allow that person's ideas, work and contribution to compound.
